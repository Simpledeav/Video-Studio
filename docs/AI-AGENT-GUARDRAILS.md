system.

The episode's full type system is Poppins (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold,
ExtraBold and Italic), falling back to SF Pro Display. The local `.ttf` files
are recorded in `public/asset-manifest.json` under the SIL Open Font License.

The male voice is deliberately segmented by scene with modest rate/pitch
differences: quicker through the apparent success, more measured at the
intervention and realization, calmer at the close. The pauses are real audio
silence, and `voice.vtt` starts at the actual word boundaries. The narration was
not sped up unnaturally; the spoken sections average roughly 150 wpm, with
cinematic pauses.

Music volume is automated downward during the realization and recovers under the
guardrails/payoff. Narration receives frame-level ducking. All media is local at
playback time.
