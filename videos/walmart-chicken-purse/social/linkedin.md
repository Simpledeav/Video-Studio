# LinkedIn

Walmart's best product idea didn't start in a boardroom. It started in the
comments.

People were already joking about carrying the rotisserie chicken bag like a
purse. Walmart noticed, made a real version, priced it at $5.97 and dropped 5,000
units.

It sold out in 90 minutes.

The lesson: your customers may already be writing your next product brief.
