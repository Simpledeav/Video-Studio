# Description

An 80-second case study on Walmart's limited-edition rotisserie chicken purse.
The film follows the joke from social listening to a 5,000-unit drop and a
90-minute sell-out. It uses the Walmart Editorial Pop preset: saturated retail
blue, oversized Poppins display, white product cards, price tags, receipts and a
hard-cut 2–4 second rhythm. Narrated by `SD_VOICE_03 — Nathan Cole`.
