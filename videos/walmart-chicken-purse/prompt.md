# Prompt

> Example record. The original prompt was not stored with the old project. This
> is the assembled brief reconstructed from the finished film.

Create an 80-second business case study about Walmart's limited-edition
rotisserie chicken purse.

- Vertical 1080×1920. (This archive film was built before the three-format rule;
  its wide and cover versions were not produced. New films must include all
  three.)
- Bright retail-documentary look: Walmart blue, spark yellow, white product
  cards, price tags, receipts, sold-out stamps, big Poppins type, fast cuts.
- Lead with the impossible fact: a chicken bag sold out.
- Cover the social-listening moment, the product build, the $5.97 price, the
  5,000-unit drop and the 90-minute sell-out.
- End on the lesson: the next big product can hide inside a customer joke.
- Use SD_VOICE_03 — Nathan Cole.
- Do not render an MP4. Browser preview only.
