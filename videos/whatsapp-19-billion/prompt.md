# REMOTION CASE STUDY: WHATSAPP — THE $19 BILLION APP

Create a premium, cinematic, visually captivating 85–90 second startup documentary about WhatsApp and its acquisition by Facebook in 2014.

**Core title:** “The App That Made $19 Billion Without Traditional Ads”

**Main objective:** Make viewers curious about how a simple messaging app became worth approximately $19 billion. Deliver a powerful business lesson for developers, founders, entrepreneurs, and business owners.

## OPENING HOOK — FIRST 3 SECONDS

Start with a dark cinematic scene.

A smartphone sits on a black reflective surface. A single green WhatsApp notification appears.

Suddenly, the phone transforms into a massive glowing digital communication network connecting billions of points across a world map.

Huge bold typography:

“$19 BILLION.”

Then:

“FOR A MESSAGING APP?”

Male voiceover:
“Imagine building an app that barely relies on traditional advertising… and Facebook offers nearly nineteen billion dollars to acquire it.”

Use a deep, expressive, natural human male voice. Nigerian accent is welcome, but pronunciation must be globally understandable.

## SCENE 2 — THE SIMPLE IDEA

Show an old mobile phone, expensive SMS messages, and people struggling to communicate across countries.

Transition to WhatsApp’s early interface from 2009.

Visual style:
- Warm vintage technology aesthetic
- Beige paper textures
- Old mobile phone close-ups
- Green WhatsApp interface
- Cinematic camera movement

Voiceover:
“Back in 2009, Jan Koum and Brian Acton started with a simple idea: make communication easier, faster, and more affordable.”

## SCENE 3 — THE GROWTH EXPLOSION

Show a realistic animated global network.

Thousands of messaging bubbles rapidly spread across cities, continents, and devices.

Show the number:

“450 MILLION MONTHLY USERS”

Then:
“1 MILLION NEW USERS PER DAY”

Use the historical 2014 acquisition announcement figures.

Voiceover:
“By 2014, WhatsApp had reached 450 million monthly users, and it was adding more than a million new users every day.”

Visuals should feel like a premium technology documentary, not a cartoon.

## SCENE 4 — THE BILLION-DOLLAR MOMENT

Create an intense acquisition scene.

A dark boardroom.
A smartphone displaying WhatsApp.
A dramatic acquisition document.
Green messaging bubbles morph into financial figures.

Show:

“$16 BILLION”
“+ $3 BILLION IN EMPLOYEE STOCK AWARDS”
“≈ $19 BILLION TOTAL ANNOUNCED PACKAGE”

Voiceover:
“Then Facebook made a deal that shocked the technology world: approximately nineteen billion dollars in total announced value.”

Use dramatic cinematic lighting and restrained financial motion graphics.

## SCENE 5 — THE BUSINESS LESSON

Transition into a clean, premium visual.

A simple WhatsApp icon sits in the center. Around it are connected people, businesses, families, and communities.

Typography:
“SOLVE A REAL PROBLEM.”
“MAKE IT SIMPLE.”
“LET PEOPLE LOVE IT.”

Voiceover:
“WhatsApp didn’t need to become a complicated product. It solved a problem people experienced every single day. And when millions of people depend on your product, its value can become enormous.”

## SCENE 6 — THE SIMPLEDEAV ENDING

Use the existing SimpleDeav branded ending scene.

Premium dark editorial background.
Subtle grain.
Clean typography.
Professional brand animation.

Voiceover:
“The next billion-dollar idea might not be complicated. It might be a simple solution to a problem everyone has.”

End with:

“Have a problem worth solving?”
“SimpleDeav builds web apps, mobile apps, and custom SaaS solutions.”

CTA:
“Let’s build something useful.”
