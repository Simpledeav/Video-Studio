# THE $120 MILLION JUICE MACHINE THAT DIDN'T NEED TO EXIST

Create an 85–90 second premium cinematic business case-study video with comedy,
storytelling, technology, and a powerful practical lesson for startup founders,
business owners, developers, and non-technical audiences.

## Format

- Remotion preview project.
- 9:16 vertical composition.
- 16:9 horizontal composition.
- Matching cinematic cover image.
- 85–90 seconds.
- Do not render the final MP4.
- No heavy unnecessary packages.
- Use existing project packages and components.
- Include the SimpleDeav branded ending scene.

## Creative direction

Premium cinematic business documentary mixed with sarcastic comedy, realistic
product advertising, and startup storytelling. The film should feel like a
Netflix business documentary, an Apple product commercial, a funny startup roast
and a premium motion-graphics explainer.

Palette: opening vibrant orange, lime green and cream; middle metallic silver,
charcoal and electric blue; failure deep red, black and muted grey; ending the
SimpleDeav dark editorial aesthetic.

Type: Poppins ExtraBold hooks, Poppins SemiBold emphasis, SF Pro fallback for
secondary text. Textures: brushed aluminium, glossy juice, film grain, dark
glass, paper-cut fruit, halftone overlays, realistic shadows, premium product
photography.

No stickman characters, cartoon people or generic illustrated humans. Use
realistic human footage-style visuals and premium product imagery.

## Scene spine

1. **0–7s — the insane hook.** A luxury stainless-steel machine on a black
   reflective table. An orange rolls toward it. The machine opens, a juice pack
   enters, a hydraulic press squeezes it. A real human hand squeezes an identical
   pack effortlessly. Both glasses fill. The machine freezes. Text: “$400 FOR
   THIS?!” Narration: “Imagine spending four hundred dollars on a machine… just
   to discover your hands could do the same thing.”
2. **7–22s — the startup dream.** Silicon Valley office, investors, a founder
   presenting, engineers, CAD, assembly, produce packs. Wi-Fi glyph. Text:
   “CONNECTED JUICE.” “SMART PRESS.” “PREMIUM TECHNOLOGY.” Narration introduces
   Juicero and the internet-connected press.
3. **22–35s — the millions.** A giant investment counter climbs $1M → $10M →
   $50M → $120M. Investors applaud. Price tag changes $699 → $399. A wealthy
   customer waits for “CONNECTING TO WIFI…”. Narration: approximately $120M
   raised; launch price $699, later reduced.
4. **35–56s — the internet breaks the dream.** Bloomberg-style newsroom, the
   pack examined, a hand-squeeze close-up, a glass filling, the machine idle.
   Error: “ERROR 404: CUSTOMER VALUE NOT FOUND.” Text: “THE HANDS WERE THE MVP.”
   State the intended value honestly: consistency, convenience, freshness checks.
   The joke is unnecessary complexity, not a claim that the machine did nothing.
5. **56–67.5s — the collapse.** The machine loses its light. The boardroom is
   empty. The pedestal rotates in a dark studio. The $120M counter fades. Text:
   “2017” and “THE END.” Narration: refunds, suspended sales in September 2017,
   shutdown. No fabricated losses or statistics.
6. **67.5–80.5s — the developer lesson.** A modern software workspace building
   an absurdly complex SaaS: 47 widgets, AI notifications, five auth layers,
   complicated settings, a frustrated owner. The screen transforms into one
   button. The owner clicks; the problem is solved. Text: “COMPLEXITY ≠ VALUE.”
   then “SOLVE THE PROBLEM FIRST.”
7. **80.5–90s — SimpleDeav ending.** Dark editorial background, grain, clean
   type. “BUILD SOLUTIONS.” “NOT COMPLEXITY.” Question: “Would they still need
   it without the technology?” CTA: “What is the most unnecessarily complicated
   app you've ever used?” SimpleDeav signature.

## Audio direction

Deep, natural, human-like male voice. Conversational, expressive, slight humorous
sarcasm, natural Nigerian accent, globally understandable, not robotic. For this
build the studio default `SD_VOICE_03 — Nathan Cole` is used. Energetic startup
documentary music, comedic suspense at the hand reveal, dark emotional score
during the shutdown, inspirational close. Precise SFX: hydraulic press, juice
splash, cash register, keyboard, digital glitch, record-scratch-style freeze,
cinematic impacts, whooshes.

## Retention

First 3 seconds irresistible. New visual element every 2–3 seconds. No static
scene longer than 3 seconds. Realistic camera moves, cinematic zooms, match cuts.
Alternate humour and seriousness. The 35–56s reveal is the strongest moment.
Clean normal subtitles. No title bars, scene numbers, progress bars, random text
animations, excessive UI, low-quality illustrations, unrealistic AI hands, fake
historical news screenshots or fabricated founder/investor quotes.
