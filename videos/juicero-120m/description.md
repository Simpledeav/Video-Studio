# The $120 Million Juice Machine That Didn't Need to Exist

A 90-second premium business documentary about Juicero: the internet-connected
cold-press machine that raised roughly $120 million, launched at $699, and became
the internet's favourite example of unnecessary complexity after Bloomberg showed
that its produce packs could be squeezed by hand. The film balances a genuinely
ambitious startup montage with a sharp comic reveal, then turns the story into a
practical product lesson for founders and developers: complexity is not value, and
the customer's problem is the brief. Built as a 9:16 vertical, a recomposed 16:9
horizontal and a 4:5 cover in the SimpleDeav dark editorial system.
